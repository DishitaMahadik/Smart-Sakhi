ONLINE SAFETY

Protect Yourself Online

Small safety habits can protect your personal information.
🔑

Strong Passwords

Use strong and unique passwords. Avoid using your name or date of birth.

🔢

Never Share OTP

Never share your OTP, PIN or password with anyone.

🔗

Check Links

Don't click unknown or suspicious links received through messages.

👤

Protect Privacy

Don't share private photos, documents or personal information with strangers.

🚨

Beware of Scams

Be careful with fake bank calls, prizes, jobs and KYC messages.

📱

Screen Lock

Use a PIN, password, fingerprint or face lock to protect your phone.

DIGITAL PAYMENT SAFETY

Use UPI Safely

💳 Remember These Rules

✓

Check the receiver's name before paying.

✓

Check the amount before confirming.

✓

Never share your UPI PIN.

✓

Never share your OTP.

✓

Do not approve unknown payment requests.

⚠️

Important!

Your UPI PIN is used to authorize payments. Never enter your UPI PIN just because someone asks you to receive money.
